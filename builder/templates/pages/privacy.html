Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Devnixplus ("we", "us") runs this website builder at {{ APP_URL }}. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the choices you have. It applies to this website and to your account on it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email, mobile and WhatsApp number, city, and your consent choices.
  • Business details: the business name, address, photos, text and other content you add to your website, and the verification documents you upload.
  • Payments: payment references from Razorpay. We never see or store your card details, UPI PIN or bank password.
  • Usage data: basic logs such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, used for security and to fix problems.

Sign in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares with us only your name and email address, and confirms that the email is verified. We use them only to create your account and log you in. We do not get your Google password, and we do not access your Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data.

We do not sell this information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except as described under "Who we share it with". Our use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can remove our access at any time from your Google Account settings; your account here stays, and you can set a password with "Forgot password" on the login page and log in with your email.

Why we use it

  • To create, publish and host your website, and to verify your business.
  • To contact you about your website and account by call, WhatsApp or email (you agreed to this when signing up).
  • To send offers and tips, only if you chose to receive them. You can turn this off in My account.
  • For invoices, tax and legal requirements, and to prevent fraud and abuse.

Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to keep you logged in, remember your session and protect forms against misuse. We do not use advertising cookies.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers we need to run the service, and only what each one needs: Razorpay (payments), our email and SMS/WhatsApp providers (to send you messages), and our hosting provider. We may also share data when the law requires it. The details you put on your website are public once it is live. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

As long as your account is active. After you delete your account we remove your personal data, except what we must keep by law (for example invoices).

How we protect it

Your data is sent over HTTPS, passwords are stored only in encrypted (hashed) form, and only our team members who need it can see your verification documents.

Your rights

You can see, correct or delete your data, and withdraw consent, at any time. Update most details yourself in My account, or write to {{ ps.support_email }} to get a copy of your data or delete your account. We reply within 30 days.

Children

This service is for business owners and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will tell you about important changes by email.
